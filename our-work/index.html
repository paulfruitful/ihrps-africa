Our Work

Transforming lives through direct action, advocacy, and community support.

Impact Areas

Our work spans across various critical areas of human rights protection. From providing legal aid to the indigent to ensuring the welfare of prisoners and IDPs, we are on the ground making a difference.

IDP Welfare Support Programme

We provide comprehensive support to Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs), including food, shelter, and medical assistance. Our upcoming flagship program aims to rehabilitate and reintegrate displaced families.

Learn More

Prison Welfare & Reform

Regular visits to correctional facilities to monitor conditions, provide legal assistance to inmates awaiting trial, and advocate for prison reforms to ensure humane treatment.

Learn More

Legal Aid & Advocacy

Pro bono legal services for victims of human rights abuses. We challenge unjust laws and practices through strategic litigation and public advocacy campaigns.

Learn More

Human Rights Education

Educating communities about their rights and responsibilities. We conduct workshops, seminars, and awareness campaigns to empower citizens.

Learn More

Gender-Based Violence

Combating violence against women and children through protection services, counseling, and legal pursuit of perpetrators.

Learn More

Policy Advocacy

Working with government bodies and international organizations to shape policies that uphold human rights standards.

Learn More
