About Us

Dedicated to the promotion and protection of human rights, justice, and dignity across West Africa.

Who We Are

The International Human Rights Protection Service (IHRPS) is a leading non-governmental organization committed to upholding the fundamental rights and freedoms of all individuals. Based in West Africa, we work tirelessly to address human rights violations, provide legal advocacy, and support vulnerable communities.

Our mission is rooted in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, and we strive to create a world where justice, equality, and human dignity are the norm, not the exception.

Image: Team or Office

Our Mission

To protect and promote human rights through advocacy, education, and direct support to victims of violations.

Our Vision

A society where every individual enjoys their fundamental rights and freedoms without discrimination.

Our Values

Justice, Integrity, Compassion, and Unwavering Commitment to Human Dignity.

Vision 2026

"With our new emblem proudly displayed, IHRPS is strengthening partnerships and expanding field operations to serve global communities better. Together, we stand for justice and human dignity."
